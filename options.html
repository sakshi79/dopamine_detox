Dopamine Detox

This extension watches which sites you spend time on and how you move between them. After two days it gives you a baseline reading. If the baseline drops too low, it starts a detox that limits social sites to a small daily allowance.

A detox is running, so the site list, daily allowance and data reset are locked until it ends.

How the baseline is scored

Each signal is scored from 0 to its ceiling. The weighted total is subtracted from 100, then averaged over your last three days with at least 20 minutes of browsing.

Share of browsing on social, 35%
Full weight when social sites take half your browsing time.
Time on social, 25%
Full weight at three hours a day.
Checking habit, 20%
How often you open social sites. Visits under 90 seconds count extra. Full weight at 40 opens a day.
Tab hopping, 10%
Switches between sites per hour of browsing. Full weight at 60 an hour.
Late-night scrolling, 10%
Social time inside your late-night window. Full weight at 45 minutes.

Your data

Everything stays in this browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Only site names and time totals are stored, never full addresses or page content. Days older than 30 are deleted automatically.
